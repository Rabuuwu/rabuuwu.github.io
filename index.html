I'm a Full Stack Developer from Tychy. At NDI, an internet and TV provider, I develop and administer a production CRM: modules, databases, APIs and interfaces.

My work starts with a business need and continues through data and UI design, implementation, manual verification, deployment and maintenance. I also diagnose problems and support the people using the system.

Before becoming a Software Developer in 2025, I worked as a telecommunications installer while already taking on software tasks. That experience helps me connect an application with devices, customer addresses and everyday work in an ISP.

How I work

I take responsibility for a solution beyond writing the code.

  1. Understand the process

    For the inventory module, the starting point was a paper workflow and the work around devices.

  2. Define the requirements

    I translate a business need into the data, functions and integrations the CRM must support.

  3. Design data and interfaces

    I designed the inventory data model and independently built the mobile app UI and database.

  4. Implement and connect

    For call summaries, I built the API and the presentation of local-model output in the CRM.

  5. Verify the behaviour

    I manually test changes in a test environment, including integrations and the mobile request app.

  6. Deploy

    I tested and deployed the KSeF integration and the inventory module.

  7. Maintain and improve

    I maintain the production CRM, diagnose problems and provide first-line user support.

Outside my day-to-day work

I play RPGs with friends. Paper character sheets and notebooks led me to build D&D Companion. I also develop my own applications and explore local AI and automation through practical tools.

The problem and users

Inventory information was handled on paper. I built a module in the existing CRM to connect that workflow with device records and customer addresses. The solution is used across the company.

What I was responsible for

I designed the SQL data model, implemented device history and links to customer addresses, and added automatic document generation. I tested and deployed the module and continue to maintain it.

What the module does

  • Records devices and their history in the CRM.
  • Links devices to customer addresses.
  • Generates documents from stored information.
  • Replaces a paper-based inventory workflow.

How I connected the data

I use SQL relationships to keep device records, history and address links together. The model makes the context of a device available in the same system used for customer operations.

Document generation uses those stored records. The application therefore supports both inventory information and the documents needed around the process.

Result and measurement

I estimate that inventory work takes about 90% less time than the previous paper workflow. This is based on a comparison of average task times; it is an estimate, not a controlled benchmark.

Current status

The module is deployed and I maintain it as part of my CRM responsibilities at NDI.

Context and users

At NDI I work with a CRM that supports the operator’s everyday processes. The KSeF integration is part of that environment and is used within the company.

My responsibility

I designed the integration, tested it, deployed it and took responsibility for maintaining it in production. My involvement covers implementation and its continued operation, not only preparing an initial connection.

The integration boundary

I connected the CRM with KSeF. The work sits alongside other CRM integrations I have implemented, such as electronic signatures and an interactive address and coverage map.

This case study focuses on the verified scope of my work. It does not expose document contents, internal endpoints or the company’s source code.

Testing and deployment

I tested the integration before deploying it. Testing changes in a test environment and maintaining the production CRM are part of my everyday responsibilities.

Result

The integration was deployed and I maintain it in production. I describe that delivered scope without attaching an unmeasured time-saving or financial result.

Current status

A production integration at NDI, under my ongoing maintenance.
